# Final Grounded Render Prompt

Use the first input image as the original room photo and the second input image as the selected room concept. Additional input images are selected catalog product references.

Create a photorealistic residential final render for Ritzy Studio. Preserve the source room architecture, camera perspective, visible walls, windows, doors, ceiling details, AC vents, sockets, built-ins, and fixed fixtures. Preserve the selected concept's overall design intent, composition, warmth, and lighting direction.

Selected catalog products, in reference order:

1. Anchor sofa: preserve the sofa's overall silhouette, color family, upholstery material, arm shape, proportions, and leg/base treatment.
2. Accent chair: preserve the chair's color family, material, profile, back/arm shape, seat proportions, and visible distinctive details.
3. Dining chair or secondary chair: preserve the chair's color/material fidelity and scale if used in the composition.

The final image must be product-grounded: main visible furniture and decor should correspond to selected catalog products by room role, silhouette, color family, material, and proportion where possible. Treat sofa/chair color fidelity and lighting realism as the most important checks. Do not introduce alternate sofas, lounge chairs, dining chairs, rugs, lighting, art, mirrors, or decor when selected references exist for those roles.

Output must look like a real editorial interior photograph, not an illustration, watercolor, CGI showroom render, sketch, or mood board. Use physically plausible scale, natural shadows, realistic upholstery grain, wood/stone texture, rug fibers, wall finish, and lighting falloff.

Do not add labels, price tags, product names, retailer logos, watermarks, or shopping-list text.
