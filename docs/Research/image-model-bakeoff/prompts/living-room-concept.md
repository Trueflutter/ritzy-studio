# Living Room Concept Generation Prompt

Use the uploaded source room photo as the base image.

Create one photorealistic editorial interior design concept for a Dubai villa or large townhouse living room. Preserve the source room's camera angle, window/door placement, ceiling line, walls, floor boundary, fixed joinery, sockets, AC vents, and structural architecture.

Design the living room as a layered residential seating group: one anchor sofa, two accent chairs if space allows, a generous rug, usable coffee table, side/end tables, warm layered lighting, full-height window treatment where visible, scaled art or a focal wall, and restrained styling with books, ceramics, trays, branches, and tactile cushions.

Style direction: quiet luxury, warm contemporary, collected-not-matched, high-end UAE residential realism. Use warm ivory, stone, walnut, bronze, muted olive/charcoal accents, natural upholstery grain, realistic shadows, credible furniture scale, and believable daylight/interior lighting.

Avoid fantasy architecture, rural/wilderness context, showroom smoothness, generic catalog staging, warped furniture, impossible reflections, text labels, price tags, watermarks, logos, or any visible retailer claims.
